Pricing & Fees

Our pricing is transparent and straightforward. Here's what to expect:

  • Standard notarial acts are $5 per signature, per document — this is the state-regulated fee in Tennessee.
  • Mobile service fee is $25, which covers travel to your location anywhere in the Memphis metro area.
  • Specialty services like real estate closings start at $100–$150, and estate documents like Powers of Attorney start at $40.

  • Rush or same-day service is available for an additional $20 — we understand urgency happens.

We do not charge hidden fees. The price we quote is the price you pay. Multiple signers on the same document? Each additional person is just $5 more. Use our Price Estimator on the booking page to calculate your total before you schedule.

Before You Book

Great question — a little preparation saves time and ensures your appointment goes smoothly. Here are the key things to consider before you book:

  • Do you have valid photo ID? Every signer must present a current, government-issued photo ID — driver's license, passport, or military ID. Expired IDs cannot be accepted by law.
  • Is the document complete? Fill in all blanks on the form before we arrive — except the signature lines. Do NOT sign beforehand. If a document has blanks, it cannot be notarized.
  • Are all signers available? Everyone who needs to sign must be physically present at the same location. We cannot notarize a document for someone who isn't there.
  • Do you need witnesses? Some documents — like wills in Tennessee — require witnesses in addition to a notary. Check your document's requirements and arrange witnesses in advance if needed.
  • What type of notarization is required? Most documents need either an acknowledgment or a jurat. If you're unsure, we can help you identify which type your document requires.
  • Is the document in English? We can notarize documents in foreign languages as long as the signer can communicate with us and understands what they're signing. We cannot translate documents.

Not sure if your situation is covered? Call or text before booking and we'll answer any questions in minutes.

Tennessee law requires signers to present satisfactory evidence of identity. Acceptable forms of ID include:

The ID must be current and not expired. If you don't have an acceptable form of ID, credible witness identification may be available in certain situations — contact us to discuss your specific circumstances before your appointment.
